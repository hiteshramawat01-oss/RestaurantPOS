# Changelog

## 1.8.0

- Bluetooth printers: in Printer and updates choose Bluetooth, then tap your paired printer (e.g. Helett BillQuick-Go). Kitchen slips, bills and the test page print over Bluetooth.
- "Open Bluetooth settings" button and short steps to pair a new printer. Choosing a Bluetooth printer sets the paper to 58 mm.
- Wi-Fi printers work as before.
- Needs the new APK (the app's Android part changed); install it over the old one, nothing is lost. Android asks once to allow "Nearby devices".

## 1.7.0

- Cancel part of a dish: tap ✕ on a line (e.g. 3 × Butter Milk), choose how many with − / +, and tap "Cancel 1 of 3". The rest stays on the order.
- The cancelled part comes off the bill, stays on the order page marked "Cancelled", and a cancel slip for just that number is ready to print for the kitchen.
- No reason needed to cancel a dish. Cancelling a whole order still asks why (one tap: Customer left, Wrong order, Test, or type your own).

## 1.6.0

- New Sales screen in the app menu: the day's total sale, Cash vs UPI (amount and number of orders), and every paid order with time, table and amount.
- Use the arrows to see an earlier day; tap the date to jump back to today.
- Tap a paid order to see its bill and print it again.
- Order history stays on the phone (it is also in the backup).
- Bill previews now shrink to fit narrow screens.

## 1.5.1

- The top bar no longer hides under the phone's status bar: it now sits below it, is taller (72dp), and has the restaurant's orange colour behind it, with white status-bar icons.
- Home shows the full restaurant name on the left next to the menu button.
- The side menu has an orange heading with the restaurant name, clear of the status bar.
- Bottom buttons keep clear of the phone's gesture bar.

## 1.5.0

- New look that follows Google Material Design 3: Material colours, Roboto type, cards, and a slide-out navigation drawer.
- Bigger touch areas: every button, tick and icon is at least 48dp, text fields and main buttons are 56dp, and the top bar is 64dp, as Android recommends.
- The New order button is a large Material floating button. Messages appear at the bottom of the screen.

## 1.4.0

- New, simpler app. The home screen shows every order in progress with its status, and a big New order button.
- New order is a three-step flow: Where (AC / Non-AC table or takeaway) → Dishes → Review. Then print the kitchen slip if you want.
- Tap an order to open its page: tick each dish as it is served (cards show “2 of 4 served”), print the kitchen slip, add items, or collect payment. When every dish is served the order shows Payment pending. Paid orders leave the home screen.
- Payment: optional discount, then Cash or UPI in one tap. Printing the bill is optional.
- Nothing prints automatically.
- App menu (top left) has Dishes and prices, and Printer and updates.
- Colours follow the Mewari Makkhan Dhaba menu card.

## 1.3.0

- Simpler app: no sign-in or staff users, and no Sales screen.
- Settings now has only Printer and App update.
- Find printers: searches the phone's Wi-Fi for network printers; tap one to use it.
- Tables set to AC 1–5 and Non-AC 1–8.
- Mewari Makkhan Dhaba's full menu (105 dishes) with Half / Full and Plain / Butter prices; tap a dish to choose the size.
- Bills show Mewari Makkhan Dhaba with address and phone.
- This version changes the Android app itself, so it is installed once from the APK.

## 1.2.0

- The Android app now updates itself: new versions download in the background and install the next time the app is closed (or tap "Restart now").
- Updates are checked every 30 minutes while online. Each download is verified by its SHA-256 fingerprint, and the app returns to the previous version if a new one fails to start.
- Changes to the Android app itself (for example the printer code) still need a new APK; the app says so when that happens.

## 1.1.1

- The sign-in screen shows the app version, so you can see which version is installed.

## 1.1.0

- Takeaway orders: start one from the Tables screen with "New takeaway"; any number can be open at once.
- Optional customer name on takeaway orders, printed on the kitchen slip and the bill.
- Kitchen slips for takeaway say "TAKEAWAY #order - PACK".
- Test builds can now be updated from Settings → App updates.

## 1.0.0

- First release: tables, order taking, kitchen tickets, billing, sales, menu, settings, backup.
